01 — About

A decade in software, half of it deep in Salesforce

Sourabh is a Senior Salesforce Developer/Consultant with 10+ years in IT, including 6+ years in JavaScript, 5+ years in Salesforce development, and 1+ year in Salesforce DevOps. He works across Sales Cloud, Service Cloud, Experience Cloud and Certinia PSA, with hands-on strength in Apex, LWC, declarative automation (Flows, Process Builder, Validation Rules, Approvals), REST API integrations, and DevOps release management using Copado and Azure DevOps.

He is Salesforce Certified Platform Developer I, alongside AI Associate, Associate and Certified PSA Admin credentials — currently consulting on client requirements and leading delivery at Zordial Technologies.
